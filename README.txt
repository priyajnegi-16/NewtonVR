PROJECT OVERVIEW:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is a standalone, ready-to-open Unity project containing a complete
virtual reality and desktop interactive physics laboratory demonstrating
Newton's Three Laws of Motion.

Includes:
- Full 6DoF VR head tracking and Touch Plus controller interactions.
- Dual-controller laser pointer rays with dynamic 3D depth reticles and emissive hover highlights.
- Smooth thumbstick locomotion, comfort snap turning, and curved parabolic teleportation.
- Spoken voiceover instructor narrations for Welcome, 1st Law, 2nd Law, 3rd Law, and Conclusion.
- Independent mass selection (1, 2, 5, 10 kg) and force control (2-20 N) for F = ma.
- Complete First Law obstacle mechanics (both Outcome A: Obstacle Raised and Outcome B: Obstacle Lowered).
- Third Law action-reaction collision physics with F1 = -F2 telemetry.
- Floating 3D VR Navigation Panel for fast room hopping.

HOW TO OPEN IN UNITY:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Unity Hub.
2. Click 'Add' -> 'Add project from disk'.
3. Select this folder ("Newton_VR_Simulation_Project_Ready_To_Open").
4. Launch with Unity 6 (6000.3.x or compatible).
5. Open 'Assets/Scenes/NewtonEnvironment.unity' and press Play!

See HOW_TO_OPEN_IN_UNITY.txt for full controller mappings and testing instructions.
