PROJECT OVERVIEW:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project is a standalone, ready-to-open Unity project containing a complete
virtual reality and desktop interactive physics laboratory demonstrating
Newton's Three Laws of Motion.

Features:
- Meta Quest 3S / Quest 3 Touch Plus controller interaction.
- Stable, rock-solid VR camera and room tracking with zero wobbling.
- Smooth head-centric snap turning (pivoting around user's head position).
- Left thumbstick smooth locomotion & hold-to-aim parabolic teleportation.
- Right controller clean laser pointer ray with 3D depth reticle (visible only when aiming at usable controls).
- Right trigger button clicking, mass selection, and real-time force slider dragging.
- Right grip physics-based cart grabbing in Lab 1.
- Spoken voiceover instructor narrations for Welcome, 1st Law, 2nd Law, 3rd Law, and Conclusion.
- Independent mass selection (1, 2, 5, 10 kg) and force control (2-20 N) for F = ma.
- Complete First Law obstacle mechanics (Obstacle Raised and Lowered).
- Third Law action-reaction collision physics with F1 = -F2 telemetry.
- Floating 3D VR Navigation Panel and instant Button B station cycling.

HOW TO OPEN IN UNITY (ANY COMPUTER):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open Unity Hub on your computer.
2. Click the 'Add' button (or arrow next to 'Open' -> 'Add project from disk').
3. Navigate to and select this folder:
   "Newton_VR_Simulation_Project_Ready_To_Open"
   (The folder containing 'Assets', 'Packages', and 'ProjectSettings').
4. Click 'Add Project' and launch it in Unity Editor (Unity 6 / 6000.3.x).
5. Open 'Assets/Scenes/NewtonEnvironment.unity' and press the Play (▶) button!

HOW TO TEST WITH META QUEST LINK / AIR LINK:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Connect your Meta Quest 3 / Quest 3S headset to your PC via Link cable or Air Link.
2. Inside the headset, launch Quest Link from the universal menu.
3. In Unity Editor, press Play (▶).
4. The simulation streams directly to your headset with full 6DoF head tracking
   and Touch Plus controller support!

TOUCH PLUS CONTROLLER MAPPING (META QUEST 3S):
================================================================================

RIGHT CONTROLLER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• BUTTON [B] (Top Face Button) — INSTANT ROOM SWITCHING:
  - Press [B] to instantly jump to the next destination in the repeating sequence:
      1. MainLobby (Central Start Hub facing HoloGlobe & START LAB pedestal)
      2. Lab 1 — Newton's First Law (Inertia track & obstacle)
      3. Lab 2 — Newton's Second Law (F = ma carts, masses & force slider)
      4. Lab 3 — Newton's Third Law (Action-Reaction collision track)
      5. Back to MainLobby
  - One press = one room change.
  - Holding [B] will NOT repeatedly cycle rooms; releasing and pressing again is required.
  - Instant repositioning of the XR Origin directly to the destination spawn point.
  - Ray-based teleportation is completely eliminated (no teleport rays, no landing markers, no thumbstick click).

• INDEX TRIGGER (Front Trigger):
  - Point your right controller at ANY experiment control:
    • START LAB pedestal button in the central lobby
    • Lab 1: Launch / Stop, Reset, Obstacle toggle, Friction control
    • Lab 2: Launch, Stop, Reset, Mass buttons (10kg, 1kg, 2kg, 5kg), Force Step buttons
    • Lab 2 Force Slider: Squeeze and hold trigger to smoothly drag force between 2 N and 20 N!
    • Lab 3: Push Left, Push Right, Reset, Stop
    • Floating Destination Menu buttons: Point and pull trigger to jump directly to any room!
  - Squeeze the trigger to activate the control with crisp haptic vibration feedback.
  - Note: The right laser pointer ray and reticle are visible ONLY when aiming at usable
    controls, automatically hiding when aiming at empty space to keep your view uncluttered!

• BUTTON [A] (Bottom Face Button):
  - Alternative trigger click! Point at any control and press [A] to activate it.

• THUMBSTICK (Tilt Left / Right):
  - Snap turning. Flick left or right to turn in comfortable 45° increments.
  - Rotates cleanly around your head's world position, eliminating all scene wobble.

• GRIP BUTTON (Side Handle Button):
  - Squeeze and hold on the Lab 1 cart to physically grab and pull it along the track.
  - Release grip to let the cart roll freely.

LEFT CONTROLLER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• BUTTON [X] (Bottom Face Button) — ROOM NAVIGATION PANEL:
  - Press [X] to open or close the 3D Floating Room Navigation Panel on your left.
  - At startup, the full panel appears OPEN automatically positioned 25-35° to your left.
  - Use the right controller pointer and trigger to select any room directly.
  - (Button [Y] and Menu Button also serve as convenient shortcuts to toggle this panel).

• THUMBSTICK (Tilt):
  - Smooth locomotion. Tilt forward/back/left/right to walk smoothly relative
    to where your headset is looking.

• WRIST DIAGNOSTIC DISPLAY:
  - Glancing at your left controller shows a compact high-contrast HUD:
    • Current Room
    • Right [B] Button state ([PRESSED - JUMP] in cyan / [READY])
    • Left [X] Button state ([PRESSED - MENU] in green / [READY])
    • 3D Nav Panel state (OPEN (LEFT) / CLOSED (TAB))
    • Total Room Jumps Executed

META QUEST 3S NATURAL HAND TRACKING (NO CONTROLLERS NEEDED):
================================================================================
• PUT CONTROLLERS DOWN TO ACTIVATE HAND TRACKING:
  - The simulation seamlessly tracks your real left and right hands!
  - 3D tracked hand models display palm, wrist, and finger positions.

• POINTING & HOVERING:
  - Point your index finger at any room button on the 3D Navigation Panel.
  - The targeted button highlights in radiant Emerald Green with champagne-gold borders.
  - A glowing 3D depth reticle shows the exact point of interaction.

• INDEX-TO-THUMB PINCH TO SELECT:
  - Pinch your index finger and thumb together to select the highlighted button.
  - A glowing emerald energy orb pulses between your fingertips for instant tactile feedback!
  - Selecting a room teleports your complete XR Origin to that lab and auto-hides the panel.

• OPENING & CLOSING WITH HANDS:
  - When the full panel is open: Pinch the upper-right [✕] button to hide it.
  - When the full panel is closed: A sleek floating "🧭 LABS" navigation tab floats comfortably
    on your left. Point and pinch the tab to reopen the full navigation panel anytime!

DESKTOP KEYBOARD & MOUSE SHORTCUTS (FALLBACK):
================================================================================
• W, A, S, D / Arrow Keys : Walk
• Mouse : Look around (click Game view to lock cursor, ESC to unlock)
• Left Click or [E] : Interact / Click buttons
• [B] : Cycle Next Room (MainLobby -> Lab 1 -> Lab 2 -> Lab 3 -> MainLobby)
• [M] : Jump to Main Lobby
• [T] : Jump to Lab 1 (First Law)
• [K] : Jump to Lab 2 (Second Law)
• [L] : Jump to Lab 3 (Third Law)
• [N] : Toggle 3D Navigation Panel (also Tab or V)
• [P] : Replay Room Narration
• [U] : Toggle Draw Mode (Unlit / Shaded)
• [H] : Toggle Desktop Controls HUD
